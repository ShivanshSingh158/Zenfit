### Movement Pattern Definitions:
- **Push (Horizontal)**: Mid Chest, Lower Chest, Chest flyes, Flat/Decline presses.
- **Push (Vertical)**: Front Delts, Incline presses, Overhead/Shoulder presses.
- **Pull (Horizontal)**: Mid-Back, Rhomboids, Chest-Supported Rows, Cable Rows, Rear Delts.
- **Pull (Vertical)**: Lat Width, Lat Pulldowns, Pull-ups, Chin-ups.
- **Legs (Knee Flexion/Hip Ext)**: Hamstrings, Glutes (Posterior chain).
- **Legs (Knee Ext/Anterior)**: Quads, Sissy Squats, Leg Extensions.
- **Arms (Elbow Ext)**: Long Head Triceps, Lateral/Medial Triceps.
- **Arms (Elbow Flex)**: Biceps (Short/Long), Brachialis, Brachioradialis.
